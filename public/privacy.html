privacy policy

Last updated August 12, 2026

This is a personal portfolio site. It doesn't have accounts, doesn't sell anything, and doesn't run advertising — so there's very little to say here, and what follows is meant to be read in full rather than skimmed past.

what's collected

  • If you email me (via the contact link), your message and address go straight to my inbox, the same as any email. I don't add you to a mailing list or share it with anyone.
  • Anonymous analytics. This site uses Vercel Web Analytics, which is cookieless and doesn't collect personal data or track you across other sites. It only reports aggregate numbers like page views and country-level location.
  • Articles are fetched read-only from a database to display content — visiting that section doesn't submit anything about you.

cookies

This site doesn't set tracking or advertising cookies. Your theme and sound preferences are saved in your browser's local storage, purely so the site remembers your choice on your next visit — that data never leaves your device.

third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may see your IP address as part of that request. That's the only third party involved — the rest of the site, articles included, is static and served from this domain.
